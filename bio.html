Who Am I?

I'm John — a systems engineer who likes software close to the metal. Over the last two years I've ported real-time vision pipelines onto GPUs (CUDA, Metal, ROCm/HIP), architected production AI systems with local LLMs, and fused IMU/GNSS/LiDAR/RADAR data on embedded targets. I learn by building: sometimes that's a flight controller, sometimes it's teaching a quadcopter to fly with reinforcement learning — occasionally it's this page, so you can put my work on your face.

What I'm Working On

An intensive deep-dive into GPU architectures: I ported foundational CUDA vision kernels to AMD's ROCm platform (HIP) and built a benchmark suite comparing performance, memory bandwidth, and thread occupancy across NVIDIA and AMD hardware. The goal is simple — know exactly why a kernel is fast, not just that it is.

How I Work

Prototype fast, measure everything, and optimize only what the profiler indicts. At Himalogic that meant replacing a failing KD-tree FLANN matcher with brute-force plus voxel grids to hit a latency target, and shaving 30% off memory bandwidth on mobile GPUs under strict power constraints. I'd rather delete complexity than heroically manage it.

Beyond the Terminal

I build and fly open-source UAVs, tune flight controllers by hand, and keep a Rubik's Cube on the desk — it never survives past two minutes. The rest of the time I'm probably benchmarking something that didn't need benchmarking.
