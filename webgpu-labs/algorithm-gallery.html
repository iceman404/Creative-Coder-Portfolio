MPM material (velocity-shaded, restarts every ~20 s)

The loop is the real material point method, in four GPU passes per frame: particle-to-grid transfer (quadratic B-spline weights, fixed-point atomic scatter), grid update (gravity + boundary conditions), grid-to-particle gather with the APIC affine velocity (each particle carries a local velocity matrix so the material keeps its spin and shear), and position advection. 32,768 particles, 128×72 grid, dt = 1/60. Brightness maps to speed, hue to velocity direction.

Reading the race: both tiers solve the same brightness-constancy problem on the same frame pair. The base tier is classic Horn-Schunck: 16 Jacobi iterations of the global smoothness-plus-data functional at full resolution. The multi-scale tier builds a 4-level pyramid, warps the second frame by the upsampled flow, and runs 8 proximal iterations per level from coarse to fine — the standard coarse-to-fine trick that catches large motions the base tier misses. Quality is the photometric residual: the mean brightness difference |I₁(x) − I₂(x + u)| over all pixels, lower is better, and both tiers are scored by the same measure. Flow direction maps to hue, magnitude to brightness.
